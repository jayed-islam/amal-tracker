Sabeq – Amal Tracker & Islamic Habit Builder

Sabeq is an Islamic habit tracking app designed to help you stay consistent in your daily worship and good deeds (Amal). It provides a simple and organized way to record your activities, track progress, and build better Islamic routines over time.

The app helps you maintain discipline in your daily life by encouraging consistency in important Islamic practices such as prayer (Salah), Quran recitation, dhikr, and other beneficial actions.

Key Features

• Daily Amal Tracking – Record your worship and good deeds
• Islamic Habit Building – Build consistency in prayer, Quran reading, dhikr, and other routines
• Progress Tracking – View your daily, weekly, and monthly activity history
• Challenges – Join self-improvement challenges to stay motivated
• Simple Dashboard – Clean and easy-to-use interface
• Personal Growth – Reflect on your habits and improve step by step

Sabeq is designed for users who want to improve their Islamic lifestyle through consistency, discipline, and self-improvement. It helps you stay mindful of your daily actions and encourages continuous progress in a structured way.

Whether you are starting new habits or maintaining existing ones, Sabeq supports your journey toward better consistency in worship and daily Islamic routines.
